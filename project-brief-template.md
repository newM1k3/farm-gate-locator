## How to Use This

1. **Copy this file** and fill out what you can
2. **Don't stress the blanks** — Bubbles will ask about anything important you missed
3. **Paste it into the chat** or say "I filled out the project brief, check the file"
4. **Bubbles reads it**, asks clarifying questions on gaps, and gets the right agents started

### Quick Version (For Simple Projects)

If a full brief feels like overkill, just answer these three questions and send them to Bubbles:

1. **What are we building?** `____________________`
2. **Who is it for?** `____________________`
3. **What's the one thing it absolutely must do?** `____________________`

That's enough to get started on most smaller projects. Bubbles will pull the rest out of you naturally.
